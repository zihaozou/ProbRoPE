Abstract

We present D-FUSE, a feed-forward diffusion transformer that synthesizes novel views of dynamic scenes from asynchronous RGB and event cameras. Our probabilistic temporal encoding, ProbRoPE, represents each observation as a distribution over its capture interval, aligning sensors across viewpoints and sampling rates. Without strict synchronization or per-scene optimization, D-FUSE produces temporally consistent novel views under fast motion and sparse viewpoints.
